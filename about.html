About Us

Our Story:
Founded in 2018 by seasoned musicians who wanted a place where rhythm and creativity could grow together, Rhythm Roots Academy has become
a trusted hub for music learners across India.
Experience & Reach:
- 5+ years of teaching experience - Over 2,300+ students have walked through our doors (both in‑studio and online)
Our Specialty:
We focus on vocal coaching, guitar, piano, and songwriting,
blending solid theory with hands‑on performance practice.
What We Offer:
- Personalized lesson plans tailored to each student’s pace and goals
- Flexible schedules (weekday evenings & weekends) and live‑online classes
- Performance workshops and recitals to build stage confidence
- Music theory fundamentals and ear‑training modules
- Community events like jam sessions and masterclasses
Why Choose Us?:
Because we believe every student has a unique voice, we create a supportive,
fun environment where rhythm is the foundation and creativity is the spark.
Join us, and let your musical journey take root!
